Halloween Rush

A free, pumpkin-powered Halloween shooting gallery

    Computer: move the mouse to aim · click or hold to fire (Space works too) · Esc to pause
    Touch: drag on the left half to aim · hold anywhere on the right half to fire · ⏸ to pause
    Controller: right stick to aim · RT or A to fire · ☰ Menu to pause · D‑pad and A in menus
    Survive 60 seconds with 3 hearts. Small targets score more. Shoot the spiders' jack-o'-lanterns before they reach you!
